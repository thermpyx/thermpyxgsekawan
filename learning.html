THE DESTINATION

What will you be able to do?

By the end of this introductory path, you should be able to:

01 /

Explain the physics

Distinguish heat, temperature, work, and internal energy; explain the four laws through physical examples.

02 /

Solve with a reason

Choose equations from the system and process assumptions, use consistent units, and interpret the result.

03 /

Test and evaluate

Predict a change, compare it with a simulator, and assess energy balances and heat-engine limits.
