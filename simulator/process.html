Equations & assumptions

Closed ideal gas, n = 0.10 mol, R = 8.314462618 J/(mol·K). Quasi-static path; constant heat capacities Cv = R/(γ − 1) and Cp = γCv. No phase change or friction. Calculations use Pa, m³, K, and J; display volumes are in L.

W = ∫ P dV. The shaded area gives the work magnitude; compression reverses the integration direction, so W is negative. Dashed curves are analytic comparisons from the same initial state.
