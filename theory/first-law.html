01

Conservation of Energy

The First Law of Thermodynamics is another form of the law of conservation of energy.

The energy entering or leaving a system is related to the change in energy of the system.

Energy In = Energy Out + Change in Energy

In the thermodynamic discussion, energy can be transferred in the form of heat and work.

02

Heat, Work, and Internal Energy

Q Heat
W Work
U Internal Energy
ΔU Change in Internal Energy

In the First Law of Thermodynamics, heat and work are involved in changing the internal energy of the system.

03

First Law Equation

The relationship between heat, work, and internal energy can be written as:

Q = ΔU + W

Therefore, the change in internal energy can be written as:

ΔU = Q − W

This equation is the main relationship that we will use for thermodynamic calculations.

04

State Function and Process

Internal energy U is a state function. Its value is determined by the state of the system and does not depend on the path taken.

Heat Q and work W are associated with a process, so they depend on the path of the process.

U State function
ΔU Independent of the path
Q Depends on the process
W Depends on the process
05

Cyclic Process

In a cyclic process, the system returns to its initial state.

Condition U₁ = U₂

Therefore:

ΔU = 0

Using the First Law equation:

Q = W

In this case, the heat supplied to the system is related directly to the work in the cycle.

06

Example Problem

A system receives 1000 J of heat and performs 300 J of work. Determine the change in internal energy.

Given:

Q = 1000 J

W = 300 J

Formula:

ΔU = Q − W

ΔU = 1000 − 300

ΔU = 700 J

07

From Theory to Calculation

The First Law connects three important quantities: heat, work, and internal energy.

Q − W = ΔU
